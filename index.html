04 / KNOW THE RULES

Build smart.
Play fair.

Review the essentials before registration. The final rulebook will be shared with registered teams.

01 Eligibility & teams −

Open to current undergraduate students of the college. Teams must contain 2–4 members from the same institution. Individual participation is not allowed.

02 Registration & fees +

Registration is ₹299 per participant. Every member must register individually; the team lead submits the complete team roster. Registration closes on 20 January 2027.

03 Originality & conduct +

Projects must be original and developed during the event. Open-source libraries and public APIs are allowed. Teams must credit all sources and respect the event code of conduct.

04 Submission requirements +

Submit source code, a working prototype, a demo link where applicable and a five-slide pitch deck before the final deadline. Each team gets five minutes to pitch.
